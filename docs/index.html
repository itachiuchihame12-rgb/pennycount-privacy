Privacy Policy for Penny Count 🪙

100% Offline-First & Zero-Knowledge Security

Effective Date: October 3, 2026
App Name: Penny Count (`com.pennycount.app`)

🛡️ 1. Our Privacy Commitment

Penny Count is built with a strict 100% Offline-First, Zero-Knowledge Privacy Architecture. We believe your personal financial data belongs exclusively to you. Penny Count does not collect, store, transmit, track, or sell your financial records to any external cloud servers, advertisers, or third parties.

💾 2. How Data is Stored (On-Device Storage)

  • Local SQLite Database: Bank account balances, transactions, budgets, debt payoff schedules, and wealth goals are stored in an encrypted local database (`pennycount.db`) on your phone.
  • Hardware Keystore Encryption: PIN passcodes, biometric lock preferences, and security keys are stored using your device's hardware-backed Secure Storage (Android KeyStore / iOS Keychain).
  • Zero Cloud Processing: We do not operate cloud databases, user tracking servers, or remote analytics engines.

🔑 3. Device Permissions & Purpose

  • Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`): Used strictly for hands-free voice expense logging. Audio is processed locally on your phone and never transmitted remotely.
  • Biometrics (`USE_BIOMETRIC`): Secures app access using your phone's Fingerprint or Face ID unlock. Biometric verification is handled safely by the Android OS.
  • Local Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): Delivers local on-device reminders for upcoming room rent, bill due dates, and budget warnings.
  • Attachments & Backups: Allows attaching receipt photos or exporting encrypted JSON/CSV/PDF backup files locally.

📤 4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Tracking, Facebook SDKs, or advertising trackers. Data leaves your device only if you manually export a backup file and share it via your phone's native Share sheet.

🗑️ 5. User Control & Data Erasure

You can permanently wipe all local database records anytime by navigating to Settings ➔ Danger Zone ➔ Purge All Data inside the app.
